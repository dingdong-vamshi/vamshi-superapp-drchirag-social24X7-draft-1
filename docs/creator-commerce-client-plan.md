# Creator Commerce Client Plan

## What we are planning

We are planning a new shopping system inside Social 24x7.

This is inspired by the general idea of social commerce, where people discover products through creators, profiles, posts and videos.

This is not a TikTok integration.

We are not planning to use TikTok APIs, TikTok branding, Amazon, Shopify or any outside marketplace for the buying flow.

The goal is for users to discover and buy products without leaving Social 24x7.

## How sellers will join

Sellers will need a proper joining flow.

The simple version is:

- Seller registers
- Seller adds basic details
- Seller adds store details
- Seller submits verification
- Team reviews and approves the seller
- Seller can then add products

For the first version, seller approval can be handled manually. This is safer because we can check the seller before allowing products to go live.

## How creators will use it

Creators can have a product section on their profile.

They can show products they own or products they are allowed to promote.

Users can tap a product from a creator profile and open the product page directly.

Later, creators can connect products to posts and reels. This means a user can watch content and open the product from that content.

Affiliate products and creator commissions should come later after the commission rules are approved.

## How buyers will use it

The buyer flow should be simple.

- User sees a product in Shop, Social, a creator profile, a post or a reel
- User opens the product
- User checks images, videos, price, options and delivery details
- User selects the option they want
- User adds it to cart
- User enters address
- User pays
- User gets confirmation
- User tracks the order
- User can request cancellation, return or refund where allowed
- User can review the product after delivery

The buying journey should feel native to Social 24x7.

## How the store will look

Each seller should have a store page.

The store should show:

- Seller name
- Store image
- Store description
- Products
- Product images
- Product videos
- Price
- Available options
- Stock status
- Reviews
- Delivery information

Creators can also show selected products on their profile.

## How trust will be handled

Trust needs to be built into the system from the beginning.

The first version should include:

- Seller approval
- Product approval
- Product reports
- Reviews
- Order history
- Return and refund process

Verification can start as a manual review. We should not promise automated verification until that is approved.

## What we will build first

The first version should focus on the core shopping system.

Recommended first release:

- Seller onboarding
- Seller storefront
- Product listing
- Product approval
- Creator product section
- Product discovery
- Cart
- Test checkout or approved payment flow
- Orders

This keeps the first release useful without trying to build everything at once.

## What will come later

Later phases can add:

- Products inside posts
- Products inside reels
- Creator commissions
- Seller analytics
- Creator analytics
- Advanced promotions
- Live shopping
- Automated verification
- Delivery partner integrations

These should come after the basic seller, product, cart and order system is stable.

## What we need from the client

Before development starts, we need decisions on:

- Who can become a seller
- What documents sellers must provide
- Which product categories are allowed first
- Who approves sellers
- Who approves products
- Platform commission
- Creator commission
- Payment method
- Who handles delivery
- Return policy
- Refund policy
- Cash on delivery
- What should be included in the first release

These decisions affect the product flow and the approval process.

## Expected development flow

The best development flow is:

- Confirm the final first-release scope
- Prepare the seller and product foundation
- Build seller onboarding
- Build product listing and approval
- Build the buyer shopping flow
- Add cart and checkout
- Connect creator profiles and social content
- Add payments and order tracking after business rules are confirmed
- Test with buyer and seller accounts
- Release in phases

This avoids risking the existing Social, Shop, Chat, Profile and Wallet sections.

## Closing summary

The plan is to create a native Social 24x7 shopping experience where sellers can list products, creators can promote products and buyers can complete the full journey inside the app.

The safest approach is to build it in phases, starting with seller onboarding, product listing, product approval, buyer shopping, cart and orders.

Payments, commissions, delivery and refunds should be connected only after the business rules are confirmed.
