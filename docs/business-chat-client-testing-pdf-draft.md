## Planned client test sections

1. Open a seller profile and use **Order from Us**.
2. Browse the storefront and add products to the bag.
3. Review the dedicated Cart and Checkout pages.
4. Open Business Chat as the buyer and confirm the storefront identity.
5. Open the same Business Chat as the seller and confirm the customer identity.
6. Send and receive normal chat messages and supported attachments.
7. Confirm order updates appear as neutral system timeline cards exactly once.
8. Open **View order** and confirm the authoritative commerce status.
9. After delivery, submit private unboxing evidence from the chat order card:
   - **Use Camera — Live evidence**
   - **Upload from Device — Existing evidence**
10. Confirm the evidence source label and private-access notice.
11. Submit a return from the same Business Chat order card.
12. Confirm the return reaches Commerce Admin and its status returns to the chat timeline.

## Expected privacy and role behavior

- The buyer sees the verified storefront identity.
- The seller sees the customer identity and the related storefront context.
- Order updates are system events, not messages sent by either participant.
- Unboxing evidence is private to the buyer, seller, and Commerce Admin.
- Live captures and existing uploads have different visible source labels.
- Admin approval remains authoritative for returns and other controlled commerce decisions.
